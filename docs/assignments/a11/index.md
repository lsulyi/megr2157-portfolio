## Motor Bracket Logan Sulyi

The bracket carries the 17.2 in·lbf torque into the frame on the motor's 69.6 mm square pattern. Each bolt loads the plate with roughly 2.2 lbf. Using the bearing stress formula and 6061-T6 Aluminum, the minimum plate thickness was found to be 0.0011 in. Obviously, we aren't using this for our thickness because it is far too small.

## Coupler Design Logan Sulyi

The coupler is made of 1045 cold-drawn steel, with a 14 mm diameter to fit the motor shaft. After setting the shear stress equal to the allowable shear stress, the calculations give a minimum outer diameter of 14.17 mm, a wall of 0.085 mm, with a stress of 73.8 MPa. Once again, the thickness is too small, so we will use a larger value.
